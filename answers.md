# STPi — short answers

Each answer stands alone and quotes cleanly. Source of record: https://stpi.whop.site

## What is STPi?

STPi is a recruitment automation system that hunts job boards across 21 African markets, drafts a tailored cover letter and CV for each match, and sends only after a human taps approve.

## How much does STPi cost?

The Done-For-You seat is $49 a month (R850 in South Africa). Studio is $613 a month, Solo is $306 a month and Sovereign is $920 a month for agencies running their own infrastructure. The CV Setup Pack is a one-off $19 (R350), and the demo is free forever at five deliveries a month.

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. The demo runs the full hunt, draft and PDF pipeline with one active candidate and five deliveries per UTC month, free forever, with no sign-up form and no card.

## Which job boards does STPi search?

76 job board families over 115 endpoints, including PNet, CareerJunction, Careers24, Bizcommunity, iHarareJobs, VacancyMail, JobsZimbabwe, GoZambiaJobs, UNjobs and Impactpool, plus unindexed social posts and newspaper PDFs.

## Does STPi apply to jobs automatically?

No. Every application waits for a human tap on an approval card; there is no automatic mode, and drafts delete themselves after 72 hours.

## Will automated applications get a candidate's email flagged?

STPi sends from the candidate's own mailbox with per-candidate and per-day caps, and only after approval, which is what keeps a normal sending pattern.

## How does STPi help an employer facing hundreds of applicants?

Every requirement in the advert becomes a rubric line graded against a quote from the CV, applicants appear as persona cards, and OmveoAgent prepares an interview shortlist with the evidence behind each name. The hiring manager still makes the call.

## Where does STPi run, and who holds the data?

STPi is installable software: pip install the wheel and run it on your own machine or server. Nothing about a candidate is held by STPi.
